I am a computational chemist at the <span style="color: var(--global-theme-color)">Technical University of Munich</span>, working in collaboration with the groups of **[Prof. Alessio Gagliardi](https://www.ee.cit.tum.de/en/sne/home/)** and **[Prof. Angela Casini](https://www.ch.nat.tum.de/mbc/home/)** on **machine learning–powered design of supramolecular metallacages** as drug-delivery systems — combining DFT, molecular dynamics, and ML potentials to connect structure, dynamics, and binding.

I completed my Ph.D. at <span style="color: var(--global-theme-color)">Trinity College Dublin</span> with **[Dr. Cristina Trujillo](https://trujilloresearchgroup.com/)** on computational design of phase-transfer catalysts and the **noncovalent interactions** that control selectivity. Earlier I completed the [TCCM](https://www.emtccm.org/) master's programme at <span style="color: var(--global-theme-color)">Universidad Autónoma de Madrid</span> under the supervision of **[Prof. Ibon Alkorta](https://www.iqm.csic.es/personal/ibon-alkorta/)** (IQM–CSIC), with a research stay at <span style="color: var(--global-theme-color)">Sorbonne Université</span> with **[Dr. Julia Contreras-García](https://www.lct.jussieu.fr/pagesperso/contrera/)**.

Outside the lab I enjoy **scientific graphic design**, **data visualisation**, and **outreach** — from journal covers and logos to figures that make complex chemistry easier to share. I also contribute to open-source tools such as [xyzrender](https://xyzrender.readthedocs.io/); more of my code is on [GitHub](https://github.com/iribirii), and a fuller CV is [here](/cv/).
