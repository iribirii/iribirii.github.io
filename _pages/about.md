I am a computational chemist at the [Technical University of Munich](https://www.tum.de/), working on **machine learning–powered design of supramolecular metallacages** as drug-delivery systems — combining DFT, molecular dynamics, and ML potentials to connect structure, dynamics, and binding.

I completed my Ph.D. at [Trinity College Dublin](https://www.tcd.ie/) with [Prof. Cristina Trujillo](https://trujilloresearchgroup.com/) on computational design of phase-transfer catalysts and the **noncovalent interactions** that control selectivity. Earlier I trained in the [TCCM](https://www.emtccm.org/) master's programme at Universidad Autónoma de Madrid, with research stays at Sorbonne Université, IQM–CSIC (Prof. Ibon Alkorta), and EPFL (Prof. Clémence Corminboeuf).

Alongside research I teach (including *Python for Engineering Data Analysis*), supervise students, and design **scientific covers, logos, and outreach visuals**. Open tools and code are on [GitHub](https://github.com/iribirii); a fuller CV is [here](/cv/).
