I am a computational chemist interested in **organometallic cages**, **MOFs**, and the **noncovalent interactions** that shape reactivity and recognition.

I completed my Ph.D. at [Trinity College Dublin](https://www.tcd.ie/) with [Prof. Cristina Trujillo](https://trujilloresearchgroup.com/), focusing on computational design of asymmetric organocatalysts and the noncovalent interactions that control selectivity. Before that I studied chemistry and the [Theoretical Chemistry and Computational Modelling](https://www.emtccm.org/) master's programme at Universidad Autónoma de Madrid, including research stays with Dr. Julia Contreras (Sorbonne) and Prof. Ibon Alkorta (IQM–CSIC).

Alongside research I design **scientific covers, logos, and outreach visuals**, and I build open tools for molecular visualization and cheminformatics (see [GitHub](https://github.com/iribirii)).
