<p class="caption activity-hero-caption">CAMLC24 participants and instructors on the steps of the Universidad de Zaragoza residence in Jaca.</p>

[CAMLC24](https://camlc24.github.io/) was the **first edition** of the workshop (run as a CSIC micro-credential) on **Cheminformatics, Automation and Machine Learning in Chemistry**, held in person in **Jaca** from **16 to 19 June 2024**. It was directed by [Juan V. Alegre-Requena](https://camlc24.github.io/) and David Dalmau (ISQCH–CSIC–Unizar / Unizar).

I was part of the teaching team. On the programme I co-taught **“Python and its scientific modules”** (with Heidi Klem) and **“Intro to SMILES, RDKit & OpenBabel”** (with Rubén Laplaza) — the opening cheminformatics block before the machine-learning sessions.

The intensive format mixed theory, hands-on coding, and seminars. According to [CSIC Aragón](https://delegacion.aragon.csic.es/el-csic-reune-en-jaca-a-una-treintena-de-quimicos-computacionales/), about **30 participants from 10 countries** joined **seven instructors from five countries** for the week in the Pyrenees.

More information: [camlc24.github.io](https://camlc24.github.io/) · [schedule](https://camlc24.github.io/pages/schedule.html) · [GEQC announcement](https://geqc.rseq.org/camlc24-workshop-june-16-19-in-jaca-spain/).
