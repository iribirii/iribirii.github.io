<p class="activity-meta">16–19 September 2025 · Delegación CSIC, Zaragoza · Workshop (2nd edition)</p>

{% include figure.liquid path="assets/img/activities/camlc25/01.jpg" title="CAMLC25 group photo at the Aljafería" class="img-fluid rounded z-depth-1 activity-hero" %}
<p class="caption activity-hero-caption">CAMLC25 group at the Aljafería Palace, Zaragoza.</p>

[CAMLC25](https://camlcworkshop.github.io/) was the **second edition** of the workshop on **Cheminformatics, Automation and Machine Learning in Chemistry**, held in person at the **Delegación CSIC in Zaragoza** from **16 to 19 September 2025**. Academic direction was by [Dr. Juan V. Alegre-Requena](https://camlcworkshop.github.io/) (ISQCH–CSIC–Unizar).

I continued on the organising / teaching team. As in later editions, I taught the opening **Python and scientific modules** tutorial and joined the supervisor team for poster sessions and discussion across the week.

The four-day programme mixed Python and cheminformatics foundations with machine learning, automation, and research seminars. [CSIC Aragón](https://delegacion.aragon.csic.es/el-csic-forma-en-zaragoza-a-50-quimicos-as-internacionales-dedicados-a-la-inteligencia-artificial/) reported an international cohort from **14 countries**; contemporaneous posts put attendance around **~37 students and 15 professors**.
