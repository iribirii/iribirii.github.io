CAMLC26

Third edition of the CAMLC workshop on cheminformatics, automation, and machine learning in chemistry.

2–5 June 2026 · Delegación CSIC, Zaragoza · Workshop (3rd edition)

Participants and supervisors of CAMLC26 outside the Delegación CSIC in Zaragoza.

CAMLC26 was the third edition of the workshop on Cheminformatics, Automation and Machine Learning in Chemistry, held in person at the Delegación CSIC in Zaragoza from 2 to 5 June 2026. Directed by Dr. Juan V. Alegre-Requena (ISQCH–CSIC–Unizar), it is aimed at chemists who want solid foundations and current practice in digital chemistry.

I am part of the organising / teaching team. In this edition I gave the opening tutorial “Python and its scientific modules”, and joined the other supervisors for the poster sessions that open the week.

The programme mixed hands-on tutorials and seminars across cheminformatics (SMILES, RDKit, OpenBabel), automated quantum workflows, machine-learning potentials, generative models, lab automation, and related topics — about 18 supervisors and 35 in-person slots (~26 h of classes, in English). Certificates for full participation were accredited by CSIC.
